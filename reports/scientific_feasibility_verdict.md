# Gate 1A scientific feasibility verdict

**Gate 0 safety ranking: CONDITIONAL — weights and CPU interface are compatible, but the current independent readout is not ready for interpretable .005m² ranking evaluation.** Both clean and VAE validation fail the prespecified positional p95 screen, and safety-score error exceeds the ranking error budget. Two nominal H12 paths cannot certify robust support coverage or a worst-case ranking. The frozen oracle/ranking benchmark remains intact, including its constant-A0 limitation.

**Gate 0B constrained expected-progress evaluation: NO-GO at present.** Images expose external-goal geometry, but millimetre-scale scoring accuracy and reliable path fidelity have not been established. In addition, checkpoint training flip probability is undocumented and this tiny nominal sample does not calibrate physical p=.2 or p=.4 expectations. The correct safety/progress constraint, no-safe-action handling and all held-out contexts remain unchanged. Producing decodable images and matching a favorable legal branch do not validate nominal probabilities, robust safety or expected progress.

**Structural compatibility: PASS.** Both exact approved checkpoint payloads passed size/blob/SHA-256 verification, archive inspection, restricted loading, strict required submodule matching, head presence checks and VAE/denoiser correspondence. Actual tensors and posterior mode behaved consistently with the static interface. A positive saved epoch and compatible head weights are structural evidence, not guaranteed head accuracy.

The bounded neural workload completed within all approved numerical/resource caps. A final telemetry serialization bug prevented complete logging; raw failure, partial telemetry and recovery are disclosed. Missing pre-clamp float ranges/future head values/OS peak memory were not regenerated. This limits report completeness and is an additional reason to retain a conditional scientific status.

These are feasibility judgments for this pilot, not findings that StressDream improves or worsens action ranking. No action-ranking experiment, full benchmark, training, steering, paid API, GPU task or outreach ran. **Gate 1A ends here. No Gate 1B or Gate 2 is authorized or started.**
